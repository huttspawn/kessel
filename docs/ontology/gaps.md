Everything still undecoded that stands between kessel and a complete ontology of the game, grouped into decode work items a decoder can be dispatched on. Built 2026-11-09 from `docs/fields/registry.jsonl`, the landed RESEARCH documents `01a1c0ce-*`, the merged earn-chain work (#403-#408), the probe notes in `docs/probes/` and kessel's reflections. Reflection ids are legion ids (`legion recall` / `legion consult` finds them by id prefix or by the words quoted).

Sizes: **S** under a day, **M** one to two days, **L** three to five days, **XL** more than a week or possibly not in the client at all. "Blocks" names the ontology classes or relations that stay unknown or unproved until the item is done.
